A little about me

Hello, I'm Aniska.

I'm a creative person from Kalimpong, India. Art has always been a beautiful way for me to express thoughts, feelings and little moments that words sometimes cannot explain.

I enjoy trying different artistic mediums, discovering new techniques and learning something new with every project. From detailed illustrations to colourful paintings, every creation has its own little story.

This website is a small collection of my creative journey, ideas and experiments. I'm always happy to connect with people who appreciate art and creativity.

With love, Aniska ♡
